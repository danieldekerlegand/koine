## Codebase Patterns
- **A fold is two acts, and koine separates them.** `docs/reference/*-fold-dispositions.md` is the
  first half (decide + reason each finding, change no clause, no version); the spec edits are the
  second. `federation-fold-dispositions.md` is the model to copy — same sections, same FOLD/CLOSE/
  DEFER rule, same "verify the attributions rather than inheriting them" opening.
- **Every DEFER states the future break that would force it.** A finding may *split*: fold the half
  the break forces, defer the remainder with its own trigger. A rejected alternative gets a re-open
  trigger too.
- **`node scripts/check-doc-integrity.mjs` after ANY doc touch.** It caught 8 pre-existing broken
  links: retiring a tasklist moves `tasks/chief/<n>.json` → `tasks/chief/completed/<n>.json`, and
  every doc that linked to it silently breaks. **If you cite a tasklist that may be retired during
  or after your run, expect this.** Fixed in this iteration for `85`.
- **Read the spec header + `docs/reference/promotability.md` before trusting a PRD's count of
  gates.** PRDs are written at a point in time; KCB's counts went 2 → 3 → 4 → 5 across 0.4.6–0.4.8.

and nothing else. V-8 stays closed.

**Spec (`specs/capability-bus.md`):**
- **§2.4 (new)** — optional per-entry transport `binding` (V-4). Two namespaces, only one governed:
  the capability *name* stays version-free (§7.1 untouched), the transport id is local. A provider
  serving two majors that resolve to one transport id is now non-conformant.
- **§4.4 (new)** — V-5 + V-1 together. Optional `version` operand; granted major readable *in* the
  token (grant NAME unchanged); resolution stated as an ordered, exhaustive rule — operand, else

  `quoted_cost` and a *quote mismatch* refusal.
- **§2.1 + §7.1** — optional knowledge-port `payload_schema_id`, and the rule that carries the fold:
  a bare `shape` is **routing** identity, not payload identity, so a consumer MUST read it as *no

  states `kcb2`, and a port declaring no `payload_schema_id` canonicalizes byte-identically under

- **§7.3c** — floor split by axis (capability major → successor's next major; koine-spec axis keeps
  one full minor, so §2.3 and KMI §4.4 do not move). **§7.3g** — three named frames on §4.2d's
  *existing* channel, each before the fact it announces; the two binding forms it cannot reach are
  stated (DEFER-D), not implied closed.
- **§2.2** — the standalone manifest is **removed at this version**. Not a fold: an obligation
  declared earlier and now due (§7.3f). §2.3's window untouched, still 0.6.0.

### Learnings for future iterations
- **The anchor slug drops the em dash entirely, and collapses runs of whitespace once.**
  `## Conformance case — the assertions…` → `#conformance-case-the-assertions…`, NOT
  `…case--the-assertions…`. Compute it with the `slugify` in `scripts/check-doc-integrity.mjs`
  rather than guessing; it is 10 lines and it is the authority.
- **A "no digest moves" claim has to be *engineered*, not asserted.** Adding `payload_schema_id` to
  §7.1's key set would fork every digest, so the fold had to make the rule id **optional with an
  absent-means-`kcb1` default** AND forbid emitting `kcb2` for a port whose bytes are `kcb1`-identical.
  The interop argument closes because a port that *does* declare the new key has also moved its
  version, so an old consumer meets the moved digest on the ordinary re-discovery path.
- **Fold order matters when one fold fires another.** V-2 grows §2.1's vocabulary, which is exactly
  the event V-3 says breaks two conformant parties. Folding V-2 without V-3 would have fired V-3 on
  publication day. Look for this pair shape in any fold that adds a hashed field.
- **`python3` heredoc + `assert s.count(old)==1` is the right tool for these edits.** Several
  search strings that *look* copy-exact fail to match (invisible char drift in long prose lines);
  the assert catches it immediately, and nothing is written because the write is at the end. When one
  fails, bisect the string with short probes rather than re-reading the whole file.
- **US-3's material is staged but its answer is not.** The dispositions page names four places where
  this fold and `85`'s meet, two decided ON federation grounds (V-2's rejection, V-5's
  refuse-for-want-of-one). §2.4 makes one explicit claim US-3 must verify rather than repeat: a
  `binding` is outside §3.1(d)'s three-part de-dup key `(provider KINP id, (name, version), schema_id)`.
  Also unchecked: agora's `registry/` and `grants/` against §4.4c and §7.3g — criterion 3 requires
  reporting divergence as an **agora finding**, and the worktree cannot reach that repo, so state that
  limit rather than implying a check happened.
- **KCB now has no minor left in reserve.** 0.5.0 spent the one that was held for §2.2's removal. The
  next retirement (§2.3's legacy URI root, at 0.6.0) is the only scheduled one; any future additive
  fold has to argue for patch, as 0.4.6–0.4.9 all did.
