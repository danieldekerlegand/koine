## Codebase Patterns
- **A fold is two acts, and koine separates them.** `docs/reference/*-fold-dispositions.md` is the
  first half (decide + reason each finding, change no clause, no version); the spec edits are the
  second. `federation-fold-dispositions.md` is the model to copy — same sections, same FOLD/CLOSE/
  DEFER rule, same "verify the attributions rather than inheriting them" opening.
- **Every DEFER states the future break that would force it.** A finding may *split*: fold the half
  the break forces, defer the remainder with its own trigger. A rejected alternative gets a re-open
  trigger too.
- **`node scripts/check-doc-integrity.mjs` after ANY doc touch.** It caught 8 pre-existing broken
  links: retiring a tasklist moves `tasks/chief/<n>.json` → `tasks/chief/completed/<n>.json`, and
  every doc that linked to it silently breaks. **If you cite a tasklist that may be retired during
  or after your run, expect this.** Fixed in this iteration for `85`.
- **Read the spec header + `docs/reference/promotability.md` before trusting a PRD's count of
  gates.** PRDs are written at a point in time; KCB's counts went 2 → 3 → 4 → 5 across 0.4.6–0.4.8.

### Learnings for future iterations
- **The anchor slug drops the em dash entirely, and collapses runs of whitespace once.**
  `## Conformance case — the assertions…` → `#conformance-case-the-assertions…`, NOT
  `…case--the-assertions…`. Compute it with the `slugify` in `scripts/check-doc-integrity.mjs`
  rather than guessing; it is 10 lines and it is the authority.
- **A "no digest moves" claim has to be *engineered*, not asserted.** Adding `payload_schema_id` to
  §7.1's key set would fork every digest, so the fold had to make the rule id **optional with an
  absent-means-`kcb1` default** AND forbid emitting `kcb2` for a port whose bytes are `kcb1`-identical.
  The interop argument closes because a port that *does* declare the new key has also moved its
  version, so an old consumer meets the moved digest on the ordinary re-discovery path.
- **Fold order matters when one fold fires another.** V-2 grows §2.1's vocabulary, which is exactly
  the event V-3 says breaks two conformant parties. Folding V-2 without V-3 would have fired V-3 on
  publication day. Look for this pair shape in any fold that adds a hashed field.
- **`python3` heredoc + `assert s.count(old)==1` is the right tool for these edits.** Several
  search strings that *look* copy-exact fail to match (invisible char drift in long prose lines);
  the assert catches it immediately, and nothing is written because the write is at the end. When one
  fails, bisect the string with short probes rather than re-reading the whole file.
- **US-3's material is staged but its answer is not.** The dispositions page names four places where
  this fold and `85`'s meet, two decided ON federation grounds (V-2's rejection, V-5's
  refuse-for-want-of-one). §2.4 makes one explicit claim US-3 must verify rather than repeat: a
  `binding` is outside §3.1(d)'s three-part de-dup key `(provider KINP id, (name, version), schema_id)`.
  Also unchecked: agora's `registry/` and `grants/` against §4.4c and §7.3g — criterion 3 requires
  reporting divergence as an **agora finding**, and the worktree cannot reach that repo, so state that
  limit rather than implying a check happened.
- **KCB now has no minor left in reserve.** 0.5.0 spent the one that was held for §2.2's removal. The
  next retirement (§2.3's legacy URI root, at 0.6.0) is the only scheduled one; any future additive
  fold has to argue for patch, as 0.4.6–0.4.9 all did.

**The read** — `docs/reference/fold-coordination-federation-versioning.md` (informative, binds no

majors of one name stay two entries and V-4 is not undone at the discovery layer. Seven more seams

a foreign grant, `kcb2` across two release cadences, `binding` vs staleness, the `removal` frame vs
§3.1(f), `accepted_issuers` vs the digest, and `effect` vs V-1's digest reasoning.

**The one finding → `decisions/ADR-0014-federated-merge-merges-attributions.md`.** §7.3a/d's
**deprecated marking** and its **removal version** have **no carrier** in §2's manifest or §3's `find`
response — MA-8's class, which the federation fold folded for §3.1's own clauses and did not extend to
§7.3. §3.1(d)'s converse keys on `(provider KINP id, (name, version), schema_id)`; a marking moves none
of the three (it is not shape, and §7.2 has **no bump row** for marking something deprecated), so a
stale attribution and a fresh one merge into **one** entry whose marking is undefined, and §3.1(e)'s
*resolve-against-the-card* rule cannot fire because the disagreement is gone. **V-7 is what makes it
bite**: §7.3g had just established that for a discovery binding the pull side is the *whole* contract.
It generalizes — §3.1(d)'s key is the digest key plus `version`, so all five carriers deliberately kept
outside the digest (`cost`, `binding`, `volume`, `effect`, the marking) are outside the merge key, and
only the two with a declared bump are rescued. **Not a contradiction** — a gap at the seam, owned by
neither fold — so nothing was folded into 0.5.0 and **no version and no count moves**; the clause lands
with counts (ii) and (iii).

### Learnings for future iterations
- **agora IS reachable from the worktree** — `~/Development/agora`, a sibling of `~/Development/koine`,
  not `../agora`. US-2's learning note said the worktree could not reach it; that was wrong, and a
  read-only inspection is both possible and in scope. Do not write there (the worktree rule), and do
  not link there from a spec/schema/registry/policy file (CLAUDE.md) — a `docs/` page naming files as
  plain code spans is the right shape, and it is what `implementability-audit.md` /
  `interop-trial.md` already do.
- **The cross-read finds things because it reads for CARRIERS, not for contradictions.** Both folds
  were internally consistent; what the seam exposed was a clause (§7.3d) whose *carrier* was never
  minted, meeting a merge key (§3.1(d)) that is entitled to ignore it. When two folds meet, the
  question that pays is *"what field does this clause ride on, and is that field in the other clause's
  key?"* — not *"do these two paragraphs disagree?"*
- **Every operand kept outside the `schema_id` digest is also outside §3.1(d)'s merge key.** Four folds
  in four consecutive versions did this for the same correct reason. Any future fold that mints an
  out-of-digest operand inherits ADR-0014's question and should say which of its parts covers it.
- **A "gap at the seam" is still an ADR when it is owned by neither fold.** The alternative — patching
  it into whichever fold merges second — puts a defect in fold A's clause into fold B's changelog,
  which is the last place anyone looks. Say *gap*, not *contradiction*, and say who it lands with.
- **Check the ADR-list mirrors when adding an ADR.** Four places, and CI checks none of them for
  completeness: `decisions/README.md` (the table of record), `ROADMAP.md`'s repo-map bullet,
  `CLAUDE.md`'s `decisions/` bullet, and `docs/README.md` if a companion doc is added. `decisions/README.md`
  also had a stray blank line splitting its table in two since ADR-0012 — worth a glance when editing it.
