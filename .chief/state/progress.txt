## Codebase Patterns
- **A fold is two acts, and koine separates them.** `docs/reference/*-fold-dispositions.md` is the
  first half (decide + reason each finding, change no clause, no version); the spec edits are the
  second. `federation-fold-dispositions.md` is the model to copy — same sections, same FOLD/CLOSE/
  DEFER rule, same "verify the attributions rather than inheriting them" opening.
- **Every DEFER states the future break that would force it.** A finding may *split*: fold the half
  the break forces, defer the remainder with its own trigger. A rejected alternative gets a re-open
  trigger too.
- **`node scripts/check-doc-integrity.mjs` after ANY doc touch.** It caught 8 pre-existing broken
  links: retiring a tasklist moves `tasks/chief/<n>.json` → `tasks/chief/completed/<n>.json`, and
  every doc that linked to it silently breaks. **If you cite a tasklist that may be retired during
  or after your run, expect this.** Fixed in this iteration for `85`.
- **Read the spec header + `docs/reference/promotability.md` before trusting a PRD's count of
  gates.** PRDs are written at a point in time; KCB's counts went 2 → 3 → 4 → 5 across 0.4.6–0.4.8.

### Learnings for future iterations
- **US-2 must land two things that are not folds.** (1) Publishing **0.5.0 IS §2.2's removal** — the
  standalone `/.well-known/kcb-manifest.json` is declared "removed at KCB 0.5.0" and §7.3f makes the
  publication the removal. (2) §2.3's legacy URI root window runs to 0.6.0 and must NOT move.
- **US-2's conformance case cannot be a re-run of today's encoding.** DR-7: `kcs:live-schema-mutation`
  came back `green` over all four blocking deltas because the encoding deliberately does not assert
  an unfolded delta. The encoding must be *extended*, and V-8 says the needed predicates are not in
  KCS §5 — use the escape hatch (declared console extensions), which KCS §7 oq1 already blesses.
- **V-4 and V-5 are two halves of one hole** ("a fold must close both or close neither"). Do not land
  one without the other.
- **No schema twin exists for the KCB card extension**, so "schema twins updated where the shape
  moved" (US-2 criterion 4) is satisfied vacuously — say so rather than inventing one. Likewise no
  relation-registry addition is needed; the V-2 route that would have touched `registry/` is rejected.
- **US-3's material is already staged** in the page's final section: four named places where this
  fold and `85`'s meet, two of which (V-2's rejection, V-5's refuse-for-want-of-one) were *decided*
  on federation grounds. US-3's job is to look for a contradiction, not to assume there is none.
