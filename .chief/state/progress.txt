## Codebase Patterns

- **`docs/README.md` is a hard index.** It states "a document not linked here does not exist" —
  every new `docs/reference/*.md` MUST get a bullet there, with a dash-clause saying what it holds.
- **Retiring a tasklist breaks every doc that links it.** `tasks/chief/<n>.json` moves to
  `tasks/chief/completed/<n>.json` on retire, and nothing repoints the prose. `guard-doc-integrity`
  is a **wall** (not a ratchet), so the rot blocks the NEXT branch to touch docs, not the one that
  caused it. Link a tasklist as `../../tasks/chief/completed/<n>.json` from the start, or don't link
  it. Ran into this from `c6cae87` (86's retire) — 7 dead links, fixed here.
- **Run the guards directly, not through `.chief/verify.sh --plan`** — a bare `--plan` under an
  agent shell reads a tty-less stdin and reports nothing. Also `git diff --name-only HEAD` omits
  untracked files, so the plan under-selects gates for a new file. Just run all five scripts plus
  `bash .chief/verify-test.sh`; they take seconds.
- **The KFT modality vocabulary is stated in FOUR places, and only three are guarded**:
  `registry/enums/modality.tsv` (guarded), `specs/fine-tuning.md` §3.1 table, `schemas/finetune-job.schema.json`
  `properties.modality.enum`, and — unguarded — `registry/README.md`'s prose bullet, which
  enumerates the tokens inline. Same silent-staleness hazard `CLAUDE.md` records for *Current state*.

What was decided, and on what evidence:
- **The plane was verified, not asserted** (the criterion asked for this explicitly). `media(audio)`
  exists in four independent places in KMI: §2's `media_type` is an ordinary IANA type; §2's `probe`
  example carries `{"kind":"audio","codec":"aac",…}`; **§6's own worked media-port example is
  `"media_types": ["audio/wav"]`** and it names `mood(knowledge) → score(audio)`; §8's role map lists
  an **Audio producer** emitting `audio/*`.
- **The commissioning tasklist's framing was loose and is corrected on the record.** It says "KMI
  already lists `audio/*` in its media types". `registry/media-types.tsv` does **not** — it registers
  only the types koine *mints* (`+safetensors`, `+gguf`/`+onnx`/`+coreml`/`+tflite`, `+jsonl`).
  `audio/wav` is IANA's and needs no koine row, exactly as `video/mp4` has none. **So no
  `media-types.tsv` row for these modalities** — a later reader who looks there has not found a gap.
- **`audio-to-audio` got a real adjudication, not symmetry.** The case *against* is stated at full
  strength (it is a filter, and KMI §6 can already type `audio/wav → audio/wav` as a transform) and
  answered three ways: (1) the enum is not generation-only — 3 of its 5 rows are understanding
  modalities, so "it's a transform" excludes nothing; (2) KMI §6 types an **invocation**, KFT §3.1
  types a **training target** — a model that performs a transform is still a model that gets trained;
  (3) with no token a provider must abuse `text-to-audio`, which makes **FT-F's mandatory
  `modality × method` admission check validate a false declaration** — the one thing a closed
  vocabulary exists to prevent.
- **It lands on its own axes.** Two columns differ from the diffusion rows: `typical_base` is small
  task-specific architectures (RVC, so-vits-svc, Demucs), *not* foundation models, and that is why
  `typical_method` is `full|lora` with **`full` leading** where `text-to-image`/`text-to-audio` lead
  with `lora`. Its corpus is paired asset↔asset with **no caption side** — carried in `description`,
  not by inventing a plane notation the other four rows don't use.
- **Checked §4.2 rather than assuming additivity.** Both sides of an audio pair are `dataset.media[]`
  entries, and §4.2's effective-egress union is over "every media asset", so the most-restrictive
  rule already covers a `local-only` target stem. **No §4 clause moves.**
- **Forward declaration is on the record with named providers** (read from `ECOSYSTEM.md` §2, not
  invented): **formant** (audio-plugin/instrument IDE — both rows; its `KftModality` must be a
  verbatim mirror, so it is blocked on their existence), **lugh** (`audio-to-audio` especially — a
  user's own recordings classify `personal`/`local-only`, and §4.2's placement rule then routes the
  run to local/in-tier compute, i.e. to lugh and not the general trainer), **agora** (both, as the
  all-`exportable` case; FT-F's admission check is its).
- **Deliberate non-mints, each with a re-open trigger**: no `text-to-speech` split (TTS rides
  `text-to-audio`; §3.1 tokens are immutable once published, so splitting later is cheap and
  unsplitting is impossible), no `audio-text-to-text` (a genuine gap, but no participant is waiting),
  no `audio` plane, no `media-types.tsv` row.

**Version decision: minor, status stays Candidate.** §3.1's table is normative surface a reader
