## Codebase Patterns
- **Verify with:** `node scripts/check-doc-integrity.mjs` (links + 3 status-table mirrors; a wall)
  and `node scripts/check-doc-links.mjs --ratchet --base main` (ratchet). `.chief/verify.sh --plan`
  shows which gates a changed-path set selects; any `*.md` selects both doc gates.
- **Anchor slugs** (check-doc-integrity `slugify`, scripts/check-doc-integrity.mjs:65): strip
  backticks/`*`/`_`, drop link syntax, lowercase, delete every non-`[\w\s-]` char (so em dashes and
  apostrophes vanish), then collapse whitespace runs to ONE hyphen. So
  `## Findings — from the downstream run` → `#findings-from-the-downstream-run` (single hyphen, not
  double) and `## Downstream results — where a real run's result lands` →
  `#downstream-results-where-a-real-runs-result-lands`. Getting this wrong is a silent dead link
  that the integrity gate does catch — run it.
- **The downstream repo is `~/Development/agora`**, a sibling checkout, NOT under the worktree.
  Read-only from here (ADR-0001: agora runs, koine records). Key artifacts:
  `console/evidence/kcs-live-run.json` (the committed run), `console/src/kcs/scenarios/` (the nine
  encodings + their gates), `console/src/kcs/assertions.ts` (the §5 predicate vocabulary),
  `schemas/src/versions.ts` (`SPEC_VERSIONS` — what spec versions the run was pinned at).
- **Reading an encoding's real content** beats grepping it. From
  `~/Development/agora/console/src/kcs/scenarios`:
  `node --experimental-strip-types -e "import('./index.ts').then(m=>{...})"` works and gives you
  every step/assertion/predicate/title. That is how the per-scenario prose in this branch was
  written without guessing.

**Learnings for future iterations**
- The PRD's premise ("nine scenarios have an empty `## Downstream results` section") was **wrong in
  the reader's favour**: there were no such sections at all, and there are **ten** scenarios, not
  nine. `kft-resume-checkpoint.md` landed after the encodings were built. Do not assume the count.
- `green` in the evidence artifact means *every encoded step and assertion passed with no transport
  failure* — it does NOT mean the pressure test came out clean. Two of nine green scenarios have
  blocking deltas open. Any prose written off that field must say so or it repeats the `passes: true`
  mistake this portfolio already audited.
- The evidence artifact records **per-scenario aggregates only** — no per-assertion result, no
  clause field (KCS defines none). So every clause named in the new sections was read by hand off the
  encoding. That is DR-2, and it is a KCS re-open input, not a defect in the run.
