### Learnings for future iterations

- **A status change has a blast radius the guard does not cover.** `check-doc-integrity.mjs` checks
  three tables and passes on a row containing the version string and the status *word* — it says
  nothing about the dozens of prose sentences that count candidates ("six of six remain candidate",
  "0 of 6 specs ratified", "four sit there now"). Before flipping a status, run
  `grep -rn 'candidate\|ratified' --include='*.md' .` and read the hits: the ones that carry a
  **count** or a **ranking** are the ones that go false. `promotability.md` and `ROADMAP.md`'s status
  line are the two densest.
- **Splice long prose blocks with python, not `sed`.** These documents wrap at ~100 columns and a
  replaced paragraph must re-wrap; assert on the boundary lines (`assert lines[370].startswith(...)`)
  before writing, so a shifted line number fails loudly instead of corrupting a section.
- **Prove "byte-unchanged" with `git show HEAD:<file>` + section slicing.** A criterion that says
  *confirm it* wants an observation, and `git diff --stat` does not supply one. Slice both versions
  on section headers and compare lengths and equality; it takes six lines and it is the only thing
  that distinguishes a status edit from a silent clause edit.
