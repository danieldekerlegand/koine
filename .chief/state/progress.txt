### Learnings for future iterations
- **A tasklist criterion can carry a false premise, and the repo's normative text outranks it.**
  US-2 criterion 1 asserted "nothing else gates it"; `specs/README.md`'s ratification gate says
  otherwise, and so did `promotability.md`'s own KINP row. The right move was to follow the repo,
  do criterion 2's work, and say plainly in the commit and the notes *why* criterion 1 did not fire.
  US-3's criteria look sound — but read the target file before trusting a criterion's premise.

  bump. `git show HEAD:<file>` → split on `^#{1,3} ` → compare per section. It turns "no clause
  moved" from a claim into an observation, which is exactly what CLAUDE.md asks for.

  iteration that touches a spec's gate must edit it by hand. Status did not flip here, so the

  future story ever does flip one.
- `docs/reference/promotability.md` states its own rule that it carries **no current-version
  column** deliberately, precisely so it does not become a fifth unchecked mirror. Do not add one.

### Learnings for future iterations
- **A not-clean re-run does not just fail a count — it can change the count's shape twice.** Both
  counts went *fold the deltas* → *re-run the folded text* → *fold the NEW delta, then re-run*. When
  writing a gate paragraph, say what the count reads as **now**, not what it read as at the fold.
- **ROADMAP rows go stale in pairs.** Flipping the re-run row to ✅ without adding the two follow-on
  ⬜ rows would have made the phase look finished. If a story closes a roadmap row and leaves work
  behind, add the successor rows in the same commit.
- **The section-slice check works just as well for "no clause moved" on a non-status edit.** KCB 25/28
  and KMI 19/22 sections byte-identical, and `git diff -U0 | grep '^-[^-]'` returning only the
  `Last updated` line is the one-line proof that an edit was purely additive. Cheap; do it every time.
- `docs/reference/promotability.md`'s "Cheapest first" list is a plain markdown ordered list — when
  re-ordering, rewrite the whole span between two entry anchors in one edit and then
  `grep -n '^[0-9]\. \*\*'` to confirm 1–6 are present exactly once each.
- **Dispositions records are dated snapshots; do not retro-edit them.**
  `federation-fold-dispositions.md` and `capability-versioning-fold-dispositions.md` describe what a
  past fold did and stay correct as history. The live surfaces are the spec gate paragraphs,
  `promotability.md`, `ROADMAP.md` and `CLAUDE.md`. An **ADR** is the exception — ADR-0014 got an
  *Amendment* because the walk confirmed its prediction, which is new information about the decision.
